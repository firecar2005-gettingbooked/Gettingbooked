The essentials.

We build marketing to communicate value clearly while helping your clinic maintain an appropriate review process.

01

Claims Matter

Avoid guarantees, exaggerated outcomes, and language that goes beyond the support available for the claim.

02

Educate, Don’t Overpromise

Explain treatments, potential benefits, limitations, and next steps without turning education into a promise of results.

03

Review Before Publishing

High-risk treatment, efficacy, testimonial, and promotional language should have a clear clinic review and approval path.

04

Protect Patient Information

Lead forms, CRM workflows, AI tools, and follow-up should be designed with privacy and appropriate data handling in mind.

05

Testimonials Need Care

Patient stories should not imply that the same outcome is guaranteed or typical when that cannot be supported.

06

Platforms Have Rules Too

Google, Meta, YouTube, and other platforms may impose advertising restrictions beyond general marketing requirements.

THE KEY POINT

Compliance should be built into the workflow—not added after the campaign.

Our role: create clearer, more responsible marketing and a practical review process. Your clinic and qualified legal/compliance professionals remain responsible for determining the rules that apply to specific treatments, claims, jurisdictions, and patient data.

Strong marketing can still be responsible marketing.

We help you find the message that is persuasive, scientifically credible, and appropriate for review.
